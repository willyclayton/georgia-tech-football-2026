# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Georgia Tech football fans who want a fast phone companion for roster, depth chart, schedule, and player history. They already know the Yellow Jackets; they want glanceable facts, not marketing.

## Product Purpose

A reusable, brand-correct GT football app: official colors and marks, verified ESPN roster/stats, and ACC/GT schedule. Success means a fan can open a player and immediately see prior schools, career numbers, and current role.

## Brand Personality

Direct, athletic, restrained. Navy and Tech Gold do the talking. No fake mascot art. No purple SaaS glow.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI sports dashboards with stacked cards and emoji heat maps
- Invented Buzz logos or clip-art bees
- Cream/terracotta editorial templates
- Purple-to-indigo gradients
